ABOUT ME

Hello! I'm Alisher, a person passionate about building impactful digital tools. My programming journey began in frontend web development, where I learned to create engaging user interfaces. Driven to understand the full picture, I expanded into backend engineering with Python and Node.js. This allowed me to develop and launch two successful Telegram bots that currently serve over 500 monthly active users. Whether I am building out responsive React layouts, exploring practical applications of artificial intelligence, or managing complex backend logic, I thrive on turning ideas into functional, user-centric software.

Technical Skills
Full-Stack & Frontend Engineering
I specialize in building complete, scalable web applications - like fully operational e-commerce platforms - using Next.js and Node.js. Beyond core backend routing and databases, my true passion lies in the visual side of frontend development, where I focus on crafting smooth animations, engaging hover effects, and polished user interfaces.
Bot Development & Automation
I have extensive experience engineering complex, interactive Telegram bots using Python and the aiogram framework. I enjoy designing automated workflows and scaling backend infrastructure to smoothly support hundreds of monthly active users.
